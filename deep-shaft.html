⛏️ DEEP SHAFT

Dig down. Fill the cart. Get out before the air runs out.

Dig through the rock and take what you find. Coal is 2, iron 6, gold 15 and a gem 40 — and the deeper you go the better the seams get.

Your lamp is the clock. It burns down the whole time you are below, and burns faster the deeper you are. Run it out and you are left in the dark, and the dark ends your shift.

You can only carry so much. Take the lift back to the surface to bank what you have — but the lift only goes from the shaft you came down, so it is a long climb from the far side of a tunnel.

Some rock is propped — dig it out and the ceiling comes down a moment later. Watch for the cracks. And there is gas in the deep seams: it does not hurt you, but it eats your lamp.

Everything you bank goes in the tin, and the pit store sells a better pick, a bigger sack, a brighter lamp and a canary that finds the seams for you.
WASD or arrows — dig and walk
Space — take the lift when you are on it
Walk into rock to dig it
Esc — pause
