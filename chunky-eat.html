🍗 CHUNKY EAT

Eat everything on the floor. Then eat your way through the wall.

🥦 🥪 🍪 🍕 🍔 🌯 🍫 🎂
Food is scattered all over the floor. Walk over it and you eat it, and your belly meter fills up.

The way out is bricked up. Once your belly is past the SMASH line you can charge the wall and knock a brick out — but every brick you break costs you a good chunk of what you have eaten. Break them all, walk through the gap, and you are on to the next level.

The junkier a thing is the more it fills you — a stick of 🥦 broccoli is 3 and a 🍰 slice of cake is 17 — but greens pay far better in crumbs, so a floor picked clean of salad buys you more at the cafe.

Watch for 🍦 ice cream and 🍫 chocolate, worth a lot but melting while you look at them, a rare 🎂 whole cake that nearly fills you in one go, and 🍲 covered dishes — could be a feast, could be all chilli. A 🌯 burrito sits heavy and slows you down, a 🧋 milkshake gives you brain freeze, 🍞 bread goes stale where it lies, and rats will cross the whole floor for a 🍪 biscuit. From floor 5 there are 🪤 mousetraps baited with the good stuff: walk onto one and it gets you, charge it and it snaps on nothing and the bait is yours.

Not every brick is the same brick. Cracked ones go for half the belly, steel takes two goes, and jelly springs you back and wants three — but costs you nothing.

Every other floor there is a 🥋 sumo ring on the landing. All that weight finally counts for something: your belly is your fighting weight, and every shove spends a little of it. Barge the others out and you walk off with crumbs and a floor laid out with the good stuff.

Every third floor there is a 🍰 cafe on the landing, and you can walk into it from the menu or a pause whenever you like. Spend the crumbs you have picked up on a roomier belly, an extra heart, quicker feet — crumbs and everything you buy are yours for keeps, so they are waiting for you the next time you play.

There is a catch. The fuller you get the bigger and slower you are, and hunger slowly eats into your meter the whole time. Later floors have rats that get to the food before you do, and chillies that undo a lot of good eating.

The rats are only ever after the food — they never come looking for you. But they will not move out of your way either, so walk into one and it nips you and you lose a heart. Charge one to send it flying instead. Three hearts and you are out.

A rat is a nuisance rather than a rival. It only notices food it is nearly stood on, it pootles along at a quarter of your speed, and it sits chewing for a good three seconds after every theft — so the floor fills back up faster than they can clear it.
