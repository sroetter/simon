🍗 CHUNKY EAT

Eat everything on the floor. Then eat your way through the wall.

🍎 🍕 🍔 🍩 🍗 🧀 🍰 🥨
Food is scattered all over the floor. Walk over it and you eat it, and your belly meter fills up.

The way out is bricked up. Once your belly is past the SMASH line you can charge the wall and knock a brick out — but every brick you break costs you a good chunk of what you have eaten. Break them all, walk through the gap, and you are on to the next level.

There is a catch. The fuller you get the bigger and slower you are, and hunger slowly eats into your meter the whole time. Later floors have rats that get to the food before you do, and chillies that undo a lot of good eating.
W A S D or ← ↑ ↓ → — waddle about
Space — charge (smash bricks, shove rats)
P or Esc — pause
Or just hold the mouse down to walk that way
