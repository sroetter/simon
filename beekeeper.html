BEEKEEPER

Grow flowers. Raise bees. Harvest honey.

How to Play

WASD - Move your beekeeper

E - Interact (harvest hive, use extractor, sell honey)

B - Open/close shop

1-9 - Select hotbar item to place

Left Click - Place item / Shoo bears

Right Click - Remove placed item

Space - Use smoker (calms bees, drives wasps)


Tips:

Plant flowers near hives for faster honey.

Keep a pond nearby - bees need water!

Build fences to slow down bears.

Harvest full hives, extract, then sell!
