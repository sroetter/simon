🏃 INFECTION TAG 🔥

Don't get infected — last clean player wins!

One player starts infected (on fire 🔥). Anyone they touch becomes infected too — and starts hunting.
Run away! The infection grows. Last non-infected player wins.

WASD (or arrows in 1P) — Move

🔵 Player 2 (2P only)

Arrow keys — Move

Speed tank: hold shift to burn it. Only runners carry one — taggers have none. It fills as you survive, faster the longer you last, and you get a chunk every time you free a frozen player.
