🍕 PIZZA RUSH 🍕

Run a pizzeria — earn cash, buy upgrades, grow your shop!

How to play:
• Click ingredient bins to add toppings, then click "PUT IN OVEN" to bake
• Click a pizza in the oven (it glows golden when ready) to pick it up
• Click the matching customer to deliver — toppings must match exactly!
• At the end of each day, the shop opens — buy more ovens, faster cooking, etc.

You start with 1 oven. Earn cash to grow your kitchen across many days.
Difficulty:

SHIFT OVER
