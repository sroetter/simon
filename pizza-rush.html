🍕 PIZZA RUSH 🍕

Beat the clock — make orders before customers leave!

How to play:
• Click an ingredient bin on the right to add it to the pizza on your prep counter
• Click "PUT IN OVEN" to cook (3 oven slots — they each have a perfect-cook window)
• Click a pizza in the oven once it's golden to grab it
• Click a customer to deliver — must match their order!
• Click "TRASH" on a wrong/burnt pizza to dump it

Tips: Match toppings exactly. Burnt pizza = -$3. Lost customer = -$5. Tip scales with patience left.
Difficulty:

SHIFT OVER
