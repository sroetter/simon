🚩 CAPTURE THE FLAG 🚩

Steal the enemy flag · bring it home · first to 3 wins

Controls (P1, Red): WASD move · Hold Shift to sprint
Controls (P2, Blue, 2P only): Arrow keys move · Right-Shift sprint

Rules:
• You're safe on your own half. Cross the dotted line and enemies can tag you.
• Tag = touch an enemy on YOUR half. They drop any flag and respawn at base.
• Grab the enemy flag (touch it), run it back across the line to YOUR base.
• Your own flag must be at base when you score, or it doesn't count.
• If a flag is dropped, your team can return it (touch) or the enemy can pick it up.
