PROTECT THE PRESIDENT

Kill the other teams' presidents — if your team's prez dies, your team is out.
Last team standing wins.
Controls: WASD move • mouse aim • click attack • 1 sword • 2 bow • 3 build blocks • Shift unstuck
GATES: in build mode, click a square to put a block down — click the same square again to turn it into a gate (a white cross ✕). Walls are solid: your team walks through your gates, enemies can't.
Spectate: sit back and watch the AI teams battle. Press Tab to switch who you follow.
Run for president: a 1 in 4 chance you get drafted as your team's prez. If you're drafted, your team dies when you do — and you have no teammate to take over.
