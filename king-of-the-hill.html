👑 KING OF THE HILL

Hold the ground. Swing first.

Whichever team has more players standing on the hill owns it, and every second they own it is a point. Equal numbers and nobody scores — so one extra body on the hill decides it.

Sticks wash up around the edges. Pick one up and swing it: whoever you hit goes flying, right off the hill and halfway across the field, and they are dazed when they land. A stick is good for three swings and then it snaps.

The hill moves every twenty seconds, so nobody camps it for long.
WASD or arrows — run
Space — swing your stick
Walk over a stick to pick it up
Esc — pause
