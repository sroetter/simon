👑 KING OF THE HILL

Hold the ground. Swing first.

Whichever team has more players standing on the hill owns it, and every second they own it is a point. Equal numbers and nobody scores — so one extra body on the hill decides it.

Hold it with nobody contesting you for three seconds and you plant your flag — the hill is locked to your team and scores double until an enemy stands on it long enough to pull it down.

Sticks wash up around the edges — five swings each. Whoever you hit goes flying, right off the hill and halfway across the field. You can also throw your stick for a long-range hit, but then you have nothing until you find another.

A golden stick appears on the hill itself: ten seconds of unlimited swinging that hit harder. Worth wading in for.

Mind the ground — mud drags you to a crawl and ice means a whack sends you sliding a very long way. The hill moves every twenty seconds.
WASD or arrows — run
Space — swing your stick
Shift — throw it
Esc — pause
