👑 CROWN CARRIER 👑

Grab the crown. Hold it longest. Become king.

How to play:
• The golden crown sits on a pedestal in the center of the courtyard.
• Touch it to pick it up. While you carry it, you earn 1 point per second.
• Anyone NOT carrying the crown can tag the carrier by touching them — the carrier drops the crown and is briefly stunned.
• First to 25 points wins the crown for good.

Controls: WASD or arrows to move · Hold Shift to sprint.
