🚜 FARM ROUND-UP

The animals are out. So is every other farmhand.

The livestock is loose in the field, and they are not worth the same — a 🐔 chicken is 1, a 🐑 sheep 3, a 🐷 pig 5, and a 🐄 cow is worth 10. The bigger the animal, the slower it runs.

Walk up to one and it bolts — get close enough and it falls in behind you. You can drive four at once, and the more you have the slower you go.

Nothing sells where it stands. Livestock wagons roll through every plot towards the market standing on the line between plots — get your animals aboard before one arrives. Your own plot always has two rolling, so there is always something to aim for; everyone else's has one.

Every wagon takes 20, and this is where the day is won: whoever puts on the animal that takes it to 20 gets the entire load — everyone else's animals included — plus 5 for finishing it. A wagon that reaches the market short of 20 just hands everybody back what they put on.

Until it hits 20 the tailgate is down and anyone can lift your animals straight back off it. Fill it and it seals: untouchable, and already yours.

Everyone has their own part of the farm, and on your own land nobody can barge you. Step over the fence and you are fair game — and every five seconds over there costs you a point.

Barge a rival with Space and one animal breaks loose — the last one they picked up — not the whole herd. Lasso with E to reel in an animal from across the field instead of chasing it. Drop the feed bucket with Q and every animal nearby walks to it — including the ones somebody else was driving.

The sheepdog 🐕 turns up now and then: grab it and you can drive a fifth animal for twenty seconds. The fox 🦊 turns up too, and takes any animal nobody is standing near — those are gone for good.

The market moves every 75 seconds: one animal doubles in value and another halves, so the cow is not always the right target. With orders on there is a standing request — fill it and those animals pay double.

When every pen on the farm is full the day ends there and then, whatever the clock says — so filling your own last stall can hand the round to somebody who is ahead. Most penned wins.
WASD or arrows — run
Space — barge · E — lasso
Q — drop the feed bucket
Esc — pause
