🐟 BIG FISH

Eat anything smaller. Keep away from anything bigger. Get big.

Swim about and eat every fish smaller than you. Every one you eat makes you a little bigger, and the bigger you get the more of the reef is on the menu.

You never have to guess what is safe. Every fish is ringed by what it is to you:
green — smaller than you, eat it.   amber — about your size, neither of you can swallow the other.   red — bigger than you, it will take a bite out of you.

A red fish catching you is not the end — it just takes a chunk of your size and you get a moment to get clear. You only lose if it eats you down to nothing. There is no darkness, no hidden anything: if it can reach you, you can see it coming.

Eat again quickly and it counts for more: three in a row steps up a multiplier, up to ×8, and it makes everything you eat grow you faster and pay more pearls. Stop eating for a few seconds — or take a bite — and the run is gone.

Every so often you grow enough to pick one of three upgrades. Last five minutes and you are the biggest thing on the reef. Pearls you collect are yours for keeps — the reef market sells things that last for good.
