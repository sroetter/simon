🐟 SHARK CROSSING 🦈

Get across the water. Again. And again.

You are the fish. Swim from your reef to the far reef and you have made a crossing — then you turn round and do it again, and the water gets worse every time.

Sharks hunt you — they come straight for you and keep coming, and there are more of them every crossing. Touch one and it is over.
You carry 16 blocks and drop them behind you as you swim — right into the path of whatever is chasing. Sharks will not swim through a block.
From crossing three the sea floor throws up rock, and that is when you are handed a pickaxe: hold it against rock, or against your own block, to break it down. Blocks you mine back go straight into your pocket.

Three other minnows swim it with you. Sharks take whoever is nearest — so they are cover as much as company.
Hide in the kelp and a shark will swim right past you, though you can barely see out of it either. Pearls lie about the water and sit in chests off to the sides: each one you carry slows you down, and each one you land is worth double. Bank them by reaching a reef.
Every fifth crossing something much bigger turns up — and it goes straight through blocks instead of round them.
WASD or arrows — swim
Space — drop a block behind you
Hold Shift — swing the pickaxe
Esc — pause
