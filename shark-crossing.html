🐟 SHARK CROSSING 🦈

Get across the water. Again. And again.

You are the fish. Swim from your reef to the far reef and you have made a crossing — then you turn round and do it again, and the water gets worse every time.

Sharks patrol the open water, more of them each crossing. Touch one and it is over.
You carry 16 blocks. Drop one in a shark's path and it turns away — sharks will not swim through a block.
From crossing three the sea floor throws up rock, and that is when you are handed a pickaxe: hold it against rock, or against your own block, to break it down. Blocks you mine back go straight into your pocket.
WASD or arrows — swim
Space — drop a block in front of you
Hold Shift — swing the pickaxe
Esc — pause
