🎨 SPLAT WARS

Nobody wins the fight. Whoever painted the most floor wins.

Hold SPACE and you fire paint. Everywhere it lands turns your colour, and when the clock runs out whoever covers the most floor wins — not whoever got the most splats. You can lose every fight and still take it.

Your ink tank runs down as you shoot, and here is the catch: it only refills fast on your own colour. Standing on bare floor trickles it back, and on an enemy's colour it does not come back at all — and their paint drags you to a crawl. Paint yourself a road, or you get stuck out there dry and slow.

While SPACE is down your aim locks, so you can back away from someone and keep painting them at the same time.

Hit a rival enough and they burst — which drops a big blob of your colour where they stood and puts them out for a couple of seconds. That is what fighting is actually for.

Grab a 💣 ink bomb, ⚡ turbo or a 🎨 roller when they drop, and press SHIFT to use it.

Once a round, at a random moment, one player is handed a 💥 MEGA BOOM — a drone in their colour that tears round the arena for 30 seconds laying a wide stripe and going off as it goes. It might be you and it might not. It cannot hurt anybody; it only paints, and you can walk straight through it. You can paint back over everything it lays down.

Put more than one player on a team and you get team-mates in your colour. Their paint counts as yours, you cannot splat each other, and you both refill on the same floor — so spread out and take two corners at once instead of painting the same road twice.
