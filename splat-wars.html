🎨 SPLAT WARS

Nobody wins the fight. Whoever painted the most floor wins.

Hold SPACE and you fire paint. Everywhere it lands turns your colour, and the round is won by whoever covers the most floor — not whoever got the most splats. You can lose every fight and still take it.

Your ink tank only refills fast on your own colour. Bare floor trickles it back, an enemy's colour gives you nothing and drags you to a crawl. Paint yourself a road, or you get stuck out there dry and slow. While SPACE is down your aim locks, so you can back away from someone and keep painting them. Hit a rival enough and they burst, dropping a big blob of your colour where they stood.

A turret sits in each corner. Stand in its ring and it sets you a job — hold the ring, paint the core, or splat the targets it puts out. Finish it and the turret is yours, and it sprays for the rest of the round — always down whichever line it has least of your colour on, so it works on the ground you have not done rather than going round in a circle. Two sides stood in the same ring and nobody gains. Rivals can take it back off you the same way.

Pickups drop all round: 💣 ink bomb, ⚡ turbo, 🎨 roller, 🚿 sprinkler — set it down and it paints a spreading circle until someone knocks it over — and 🧨 ink mines, which sit quietly in your colour and burst into a wide splat when a rival treads on one. SHIFT uses whatever you are holding.

Once a round a 💥 MEGA BOOM crate drops in the middle and the first one to it keeps it — a drone in their colour that tears across the arena for 30 seconds laying a stripe. Rounds of 3 minutes or more get two drops. Neither the drone nor the mines can hurt anybody; they only paint, and you can paint back over everything they lay down.

The last 30 seconds count double: any floor you take in that time and still hold at the whistle is worth twice as much. Rounds are won and lost right at the end.

Every round pays you 🪙 paint tokens — +10 a burst and −5 every time you go down, for the floor you finish holding, for winning, for every turret you still hold and for claiming the mega boom. The 🛒 paint shop sells a bigger tank, a faster drink, a wider spray, quicker feet, more pickups, and — for a great many rounds' worth — a corner turret that is already yours at kickoff. None of it is cheap on purpose.

Put more than one player on a team and you get team-mates in your colour. Their paint counts as yours and you cannot splat each other. On the big arena the floor is twice the window each way — the map in the corner shows the whole fight.

🛒 PAINT SHOP

Nothing here is cheap. Most of it takes a great many rounds.

You bank paint tokens for every round you play — the more floor you finish holding, the more you take away. Winning, holding turrets and claiming the mega boom all pay on top. What you buy here is yours for good.
