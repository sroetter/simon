WICK

You are a candle. It is a long night.

Your flame is the only light there is, and it is burning down the whole time. That ring of light is three things at once: how far you can see, how far your weapons reach, and how much life you have left. Let it gutter out and the night has you.

Everything in the dark is drawn to light. Burn them and they drop wax — walk over it to feed the flame and to grow. The catch is the one that makes this game: a bigger flame draws a bigger crowd. Burn low and few things find you, but you are weak and half blind. Burn bright and the whole night comes at once.

Every time you grow you pick one of three — a new way to burn, or more of one you already have. Get eight of anything and it changes into something else entirely.

Last six minutes and the sun comes up. Whatever tallow you carry out is yours to keep, and the chandler sells things that last for good.
W A S D or ← ↑ ↓ → — drift about
Shift — flare: burn bright, spend wax
P or Esc — pause
Everything fires on its own. Just move.
