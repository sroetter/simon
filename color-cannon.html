COLOR CANNON

Tubes hang from the ceiling and spit out colored blocks. Your bars are at the bottom — let 5 blocks through and it's game over. The bars are repaired at the start of every round.

← → one press moves you exactly one bar  •  SPACE fire  •  P pause

Every bar sits under its own tube — purple under purple, green under green — and your shot takes the color of the bar you're parked on. Blocks drift as they fall, so hitting one with its matching color sets off a chain blast and scores double.

Every tube has 3 lives. Wipe them all out to clear the level. Each level is a little faster, and every 3rd level adds a new tube and bar — but the speed resets.
