📦 CARGO RUN

Everyone wants the same crates. Only one of you gets paid.

Crates drop all over the yard and they are not worth the same — a plain crate is 1, a blue chest 3, a purple safe 5, and gold bullion is worth 10. The number is written on the lid.

Carry up to four at once — each one slows you down — and load them onto one of the three platforms in the middle.

Here is the catch: loading a platform does not pay you. A platform only pays out when it reaches its quota — 12, 20 and 30 — and until then everything sitting on it can be stolen straight back off it by anybody who drives over it. Once the quota is met the platform locks, everyone who contributed is paid what they put in, and it is safe for good.

Ram with Space and they drop their whole load on the floor. Miss and you are on a cooldown, wide open. Boost pads fling you across the yard.

Lock all three platforms and the shift ends early.
WASD or arrows — drive
Space — ram
Drive onto a platform to load or raid
Esc — pause
