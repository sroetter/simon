LASER TAG ARENA

WASD/arrows to move  •  mouse aims  •  click or space to shoot  •  R reloads
Two to ten sides, or nobody's team but your own. One hit is a tag — in a timed match it just goes on the board and you keep playing; in knockout it ends your round; in takeover it puts you on the other side.
Tagged means turned — you start shooting for whoever tagged you. A side wins by absorbing everybody — but you only win if you finish in the colour you started in. Get turned back to your own colour and it counts again.
Most hits wins — but every hit you take costs you half a point.
